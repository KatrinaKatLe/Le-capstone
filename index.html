My Upcoming Concert

A concert tour that I will go to soon.

What Is the Tour?

This tour brings three franchises together for one concert experience: Descendants, Zombies, and Camp Rock.

Who Is on the Tour?

Descendants: Dara Reneé, Kiara Romero, Alexandro Byrd, Liamani Segura
Zombies: Malachi Barton, Mekonnen Knife, Swayam Bhatia
Camp Rock: Malachi Barton, Liamani Segura, Hudson Stone

What I Am Excited For

I am excited to see the cast live and hear the songs on stage.

Why I Am Going

I am going because I want to see three of the cast members who are going. This is my second time going on this tour, and it feels like a once-in-a-lifetime chance to go.
